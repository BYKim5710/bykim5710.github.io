24 Questions of Chairman Byung-chul Lee

Fundamental Questions on Religion Raised by the Founder of Samsung Group

Byung Youn Kim answers the 24 fundamental questions on God, religion, humanity, death, and the afterlife raised by Samsung Group founder Chairman Byung-chul Lee. Click a question below to go directly to the corresponding answer.

Some questions contain extensive material, so their answers are divided into two or three parts.
01 How can the existence of God be proven? Why does God not clearly reveal His existence? 02 God is said to be the Creator of all things in the universe. How can this be proven? 03 Biologists say that human beings are also products of a long process of evolution. How does this differ from God's creation of humanity? Are human beings and other living creatures not also products of evolution? 04 It seems that someday the synthesis of life and even an age of extraordinary longevity may become possible. If science continues to advance without limit, would this not eventually negate the existence of God? 05 If God loves human beings, why did He give them suffering, misfortune, and death? 06 Why did God create evil people? For example, Hitler, Stalin, and various brutal criminals. 07 Jesus is said to have died to atone for our sins. What are our sins? Why did God allow us to commit sin? 08 How was the Bible compiled? How can it be proven that it is the Word of God? 09 What is religion? Why do human beings need it? 10 What is the soul? 11 What are the different types of religion and their characteristics? 12 Is it impossible to go to Heaven without believing in Catholicism? There are many good people among the nonreligious, atheists, and followers of other religions. Where do they go after death? 13 If the purpose of all religions is to help people live good lives, why is Catholicism regarded as the best while other religions are treated as heretical? 14 How can we believe that after a person dies, the soul does not die but goes to Heaven or Hell? 15 Many people enjoy wealth and prosperity without faith, and even many evil people live in wealth and comfort. What lesson does God intend through this? 16 The Bible compares a rich person's entering Heaven to a camel passing through the eye of a needle. Does this mean that the rich are evil? 17 In a country such as Italy, where 99% of the population is said to be Catholic, why are there so much social disorder and crime, and why has it not become a model nation for the world? 18 People of faith sometimes become like fanatics. How is this different from Communist Party members becoming fanatically devoted to communism? 19 Catholicism and communism are said to be fundamentally opposed. Why, then, did countries with many Catholics become communist states? Examples include Poland and other Eastern European countries, and Nicaragua. 20 In Korea, churches seem to be everywhere and there are many believers. Why, then, are there still so much crime and so many social problems? 21 It is said that the decisions of the Pope of Rome cannot be wrong. But since he is also human, how can such an absolute claim be possible? 22 What kind of person is a Catholic priest? Why is he celibate? What kind of person is a nun? Why is she celibate? 23 Some Catholic organizations define business owners as exploiters and workers as the exploited, and encourage division and destruction within companies. Does this mean that they reject the capitalist system and its virtues? 24 Will the end of the world come?
